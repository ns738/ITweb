Ⅱ Evolution of IT

IT has evolved from room‑sized mainframes to cloud‑native microservices and AI‑driven automation. Follow the timeline and see how each era reshaped how we build, secure, and scale systems.

1960s–1980s
Mainframes & Early Networks
Centralized computing, batch processing, and the birth of local networks. IT teams focused on uptime, physical hardware, and proprietary systems.
1990s–2000s
Client‑Server & the Web
PCs, LANs, and the rise of the internet. Web servers, relational databases, and early cybersecurity practices became standard in IT departments.
2010s
Cloud, Mobile, & DevOps
Virtualization, public cloud, smartphones, and DevOps culture transformed how software is delivered—continuous integration and deployment became the norm.
2020s+
AI‑Driven, Zero‑Trust, & Edge
AI/ML, zero‑trust security, edge computing, and container orchestration define modern IT. Automation and observability are now core capabilities.
